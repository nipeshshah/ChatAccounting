Bookkeeping fails when it feels like a chore.

Notebooks get lost, spreadsheets get skipped, and full accounting software was built for accountants. Chat Accountant is built for the person actually running the counter.

Credit gets forgotten

Every credit sale and purchase updates the debtor or creditor automatically, and a statement is one tap away.

Bills take too long

Invoices print on a 3-inch receipt roll or A4, or go straight to the customer's WhatsApp.

Mistakes pile up

Every entry is reviewed before saving and can be undone for a few seconds afterwards.

From tap to bill in four steps.

  1. ChooseIncome, expense, sale, purchase, received or paid.
  2. AnswerCash or credit, amount, GST rate. Prefilled where it can be.
  3. ReviewOne line shows what will be saved. Edit anything, or save.
  4. SendPrint the invoice or send it on WhatsApp. Undo is a tap away.

Who can use it, and for what.

Anyone who sells, buys or spends money and wants a clear picture without learning accounting software.

Shopkeepers and traders
Cash and UPI sales through the day, credit to regular customers, supplier purchases with GST, and a daily cash view.
Wholesalers and distributors
Debtor and creditor ledgers, cash discounts on part payment, statements for customers who dispute balances.
Freelancers and consultants
Invoice clients, track who has paid, log expenses, and see this month and this financial year at a glance.
Service businesses
Salons, clinics, repair shops and coaching classes: quick receipts, bills on a thermal printer, and monthly expense tracking.
Contractors and small manufacturers
Purchases against supplier invoices, money owed by clients, and GST-ready sales and purchase totals.
Home and online sellers
Orders taken on WhatsApp or Instagram: record the sale, send the bill back in the same chat.
Bookkeepers and accountants
Help small clients keep clean books, export CSV, and use statements and reports during reviews.

What it does today.

Chat-style entry

Sales, purchases, income, expenses, receipts and payments, each in a few taps, cash or credit.

GST when you need it

Switch GST on for sales or leave it off. CGST + SGST or IGST is worked out from the states. Purchases can always carry GST.

Debtors and creditors

Balances update on every credit entry. Part payments within 5% can be booked as a cash discount.

Statements

Debtor and creditor statements by period, with invoice numbers, printable or shareable as PDF.

Bills your way

3-inch thermal, A4 or A5 layouts. Sale invoices are numbered in your own format, with the amount in words.

WhatsApp and PDF sharing

Send the bill exactly as generated, straight to the customer's chat.

Reporting

Today, this week, this month or a financial year: sales, purchases, income, expenses, money received and spent, by category.

Review and undo

Check each entry before it is saved and undo it afterwards, including the request sent to the other party.

Requests between businesses

Send a transaction to a customer or supplier's verified mobile number. If they accept, it lands in their books too.

Made for phones

Large tap targets, Indian number format (₹1,23,456.00), full-screen chat and a quick setup wizard.

Your data, protected

Each account sees only its own records, with an optional PIN lock and CSV export any time.

Flexible views

Choose what the chat totals show: cash, sales and purchases, income and expense, or debt and credit.
