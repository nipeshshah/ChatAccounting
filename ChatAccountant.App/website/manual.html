1. Getting started

  1. Open the app and tap Continue with Google.
  2. On first use a short setup starts. It asks whether you charge GST, then your business name, address and state (plus GSTIN and GST rates if you charge GST), and your payment accounts such as Cash and UPI. You can tap Skip for now and finish later in Settings.
  3. You land in the chat. Tap a button to start recording.

2. The chat and quick menu

You never type long entries. The app asks one question at a time and shows buttons. Type only when it asks for a name, amount or date.

Quick menu

Log Income / Log Expense
Money that is not a sale or purchase, for example rent, fees or interest.
Received / Paid
Settle what a debtor owes you, or what you owe a creditor.
Sale / Purchase
Invoices, cash or credit. Sales use GST only if GST is on in Settings.
Print Bill
Print or send a bill for a past sale.
Set Date / Reset Date
Record transactions on another date. A banner shows the date in use. Tap Reset Date to return to today.
Statement
A statement for a debtor, a creditor, or your income and expenses. It opens on today, with buttons to change the period, print or share.
View
Balances of your debtors and creditors. The transaction list now lives in Statement > Income & Expense.
Export CSV
Download or share your ledger as a spreadsheet file.

Top cards

The three cards show today only. By default they show cash received, cash spent and the net. Change what they show in Settings under Chat totals. Tap Reporting above them for longer periods.

Full screen

When you start a transaction the chat goes full screen. To return, open the menu and tap Exit Full Screen, or tap the small icon at the right of the slim header. This works even in the middle of a transaction.

Review and undo

  • Before anything is saved you see a line such as ₹1,180.00 · Sale · Cash with Save and Edit. Edit lets you pick the part to change, then shows the review again.
  • After saving, a bar appears for about 7 seconds with Undo. It removes the entry, restores balances and withdraws a request sent to the other party, if they have not answered yet.
  • The cancel (X) button beside the message box stops the current transaction. Nothing is saved.

3. Recording transactions

Log Income and Log Expense

  1. Choose Cash or Credit.
  2. Cash: pick the payment mode. Credit: pick the debtor (income) or creditor (expense), or add a new one.
  3. Pick a category, enter the amount and an optional description, review and save.

Credit raises that person's balance. Settle it later with Received or Paid.

Sale

  1. Choose Credit (pick the debtor) or Cash (pick the payment mode).
  2. Enter the amount. If GST is on, choose the rate. When only one rate is saved, it is already filled in; just tap send.
  3. Add a description, review and save. The invoice number is fixed at this point and shown in the review.
  4. Choose Print bill, Share bill, WhatsApp or No thanks.

With GST on, the app splits tax into CGST and SGST for the same state, or IGST for a different state, using the states saved for you and the customer.

Purchase

Purchases always allow GST. You must enter the supplier's invoice number and the invoice date. The date starts as today and can be changed. The purchase is booked on that date. If the same supplier invoice number was already entered, the review shows a warning.

Received and Paid

  1. Choose From a Debtor (or To a Creditor), then the person and the payment mode.
  2. The amount is filled in with what is due. Change it if they paid less or more.
  3. If the amount is short by up to 5%, the app asks whether the gap is a discount. Yes, discount clears the full balance and records the discount separately. No, carry forward leaves the gap on their balance.
Quick amount buttonsUnder the amount box: round up, round down, +1, +5, +10, +100 and more. They act on the number in the box.

4. Debtors and creditors

A debtor owes you money. A creditor is someone you owe. Open the Manage tab to see them.

  • Add a person with name, opening balance, state, address, GSTIN and mobile number (all optional except the name).
  • Edit or delete with the pencil and bin icons. Deleting does not change past entries.
  • Statement (scroll icon) opens their statement.
  • Sync (circular arrows) fetches their name, address, GSTIN and state if their mobile number belongs to another Chat Accountant user who shares a business profile. Sync all does this for the whole list.

The same tab holds Categories (income and expense) and Accounts (cash, UPI, bank and so on). Tap the cross on a chip to remove one.

5. Bills and statements

Choose your paper

In Settings, Print size offers 3 Inch (thermal receipt printer), A4 and A5. Bills and statements are laid out for the size you pick.

Print, share or WhatsApp

  • Print opens your printer dialog.
  • Share makes a PDF and, on a phone or tablet, opens the share sheet. On a computer the PDF is saved to your Downloads folder, ready to attach to an email or WhatsApp.
  • WhatsApp opens the customer's chat with the bill link ready. If their number is not saved it asks for it. Tap Send inside WhatsApp. Links work for 30 days. In demo mode there is nowhere to host the file, so it uses the share sheet instead.

Statements

Choose Statement in the quick menu, or the scroll icon on a person in Manage. In the quick menu you pick Debtor, Creditor or Income & Expense.

  • The statement for today appears straight away. Buttons under it switch to This week, This month, Last month, All time or a Custom range, and offer Print and Share.
  • Debtor and creditor statements show the invoice number on each line, DR or CR, and the amount. The balance is shown at the end.
  • Income & Expense lists every transaction in the period, with the date once per day, the amount in or out, and totals for in, out and net.

6. Reporting

Open the Reporting tab and pick Today, This week, This month, Current FY or Last FY. Weeks run Monday to Sunday. The financial year runs 1 April to 31 March.

7. Requests between businesses

If a debtor or creditor has a mobile number saved, each transaction with them is sent as a request. If they accept, the matching entry is added to their own books. Nothing is added until they accept.

  1. Verify your own number in Profile: enter it, tap Send code, then enter the code you receive.
  2. Save mobile numbers on your debtors and creditors.
  3. Check Requests. A red badge shows how many are waiting. Tap Accept or Decline.

A request can be answered only once. You can withdraw one with Undo if the other person has not answered.

8. Settings

Business Profile
Name, address, state and GSTIN printed on bills. A tick box controls whether people with your verified number can fetch these details.
Invoice Numbering
Use [INDEX] for the number, for example AMB/2025-26/[INDEX]. Set the next number to continue from your old series.
GST on sales
Off means sales ask no GST questions and print as a plain invoice. Purchases can still carry GST.
GST Rates
The rates offered when you pick a rate. With one rate saved, it is filled in for you.
Chat totals
What the top cards show: Cash, Sale / Purchase, Income / Expense or Debt / Credit.
Quick menu view
Regular shows a short description under each button. Compact shows only the icon and title.
Print size
3 Inch, A4 or A5.
Number & currency
Indian grouping (1,23,456.00) or international (123,456.00), and the currency symbol.
App Lock (PIN)
A 4-digit PIN that locks the app screen on this device. It does not encrypt your data.

9. Your data and safety

  • Your records are saved to your account. Each account sees only its own data.
  • The file icon at the top right of the header (or Export CSV in the quick menu) downloads your ledger as a CSV. Export regularly as a backup.
  • Sign out is in Profile.
Reset deletes everythingThe circular arrow next to it in the header erases all transactions, customers and settings for your account. It cannot be undone, so export a CSV first.

10. Troubleshooting

The sale does not ask for GST.
GST on sales is switched off. Turn it on in Settings.
Cash totals differ from what I expected.
The top cards show today only, and in Cash mode they leave out credit entries. Check Chat totals in Settings, or open Reporting.
Request or sync says my number is not verified.
Verify your mobile number in Profile first.
WhatsApp opens without the bill.
You may be in demo mode, or the upload failed. Use Share instead and choose WhatsApp there.
The browser blocks WhatsApp from opening.
Tap the Open WhatsApp button that appears.
A PDF looks wrong or empty.
Try again with a connection, or tap Save PDF and open the file. If it repeats, contact us with your phone and browser name.
An email says the attachment is empty or "no_name".
Sharing straight from a computer into email apps can send an empty file, so on a computer the app now saves the PDF to Downloads instead. Attach that file to your email yourself.
I saved a wrong entry.
Tap Undo straight away. Later, enter a correcting transaction. Past entries cannot be edited yet.

11. Terms used

Debtor
A person or business that owes you money.
Creditor
A person or business you owe money to.
DR / CR
Debit and credit. For a debtor, a sale is DR and a payment received is CR. For a creditor, a purchase is CR and a payment made is DR.
Carry forward
A small unpaid amount left on the balance for next time.
GSTIN
The 15-character GST registration number.
CGST, SGST, IGST
Central and State GST apply within one state. IGST applies between states.
Financial year
1 April to 31 March.
